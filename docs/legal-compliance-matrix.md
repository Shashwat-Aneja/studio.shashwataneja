### Address and grievance designation

The currently applicable [2020 E-Commerce Rules](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/public/upload/files/E%20commerce%20rules_1732703966.pdf), if Studio's offering falls within scope, require a principal geographic address and grievance/contact disclosures. City-only Delhi cannot be represented as satisfying that requirement. An enquiry-only flow is not a reliable exemption. Complete business address and any required phone disclosures therefore remain launch blockers pending applicability advice and an owner-approved lawful correspondence solution. No residential address was retrieved or published.

Rule4(4) does not state an independent/officer-qualification requirement barring an operator from handling grievances. The owner is designated locally as customer Grievance Officer and privacy contact, with support distinguished by purpose. This is an inference from the rule text, not a legal eligibility certificate or DPO appointment. The [official parliamentary explanation of SPDI grievance duties](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/175/AU1522.pdf?source=pqals) supports the name/contact and one-month redress requirement where applicable; the old final SPDI PDF endpoint returned 404, so counsel should revalidate the current final instrument. Do not substitute the intermediary rules for SPDI rules.

The [2021 amendment](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/public/upload/files/Consumer%20Protection%20(E-Commerce)%20(Amendment)%20Rules,%202021_1732704241.pdf) replaces Rule4(1) with a conditional resident nodal-officer requirement. The original provision is not a blanket current incorporation requirement for every sole operator. Studio's legal form beyond individual operator is not independently registered/verified here. The 2026 amendment's official search extract confirms 1 January 2027 commencement; direct PDF retrieval timed out. Recheck its full text before that date, including complaint-copy/NCH and self-audit duties where applicable.

### Retention and current operational duties

See [retention procedure](data-retention-deletion.md): six months, manually managed, no automatic deletion claim. Operating a commercial digital-service business is not established to be exempt merely because the owner is an individual.
