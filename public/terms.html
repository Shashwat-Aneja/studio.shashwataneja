Clear scope.
Clear expectations.

Email-only booking requests · Updated 14 November 2026

1. Indicative estimates

Studio's prices, project breakdowns and timelines are planning estimates. Final deliverables, assumptions, revisions, milestones, taxes, third-party costs and availability must be agreed in a written quotation before work begins.

2. Provisional booking requests

Submitting the form sends your request and provisional BUILD DETAILS PDF to Studio for manual review. A success message means the email provider accepted the message; it does not confirm inbox delivery, project approval or a binding quotation. Studio will review your requirements and contact you. No response deadline is promised.

3. Payments and invoices

No payment is collected by this website. The provisional PDF is not a paid invoice or tax invoice, and nothing is payable at submission. Any future payment method, advance, milestones, cancellation or refund terms must be agreed separately in the approved quotation. A finalized invoice is not automatically emailed to you.

4. Scope changes and responsibilities

Provide accurate contact and project details. Changes to deliverables, integrations, content, revisions or delivery dates may change the estimate and schedule and must be agreed before extra work begins. Only provide lawful access to systems you control; never put credentials or card details in the form.

5. Third-party costs

Domains, hosting, licences, subscriptions and other external costs are separate unless the written quotation explicitly includes them. Third-party terms and availability may apply.

6. Submission failures and retries

If receipt is uncertain, keep your Build ID and retry the unchanged request. The provider's duplicate protection is time-limited and cannot guarantee exactly-once delivery. After the retry window expires, contact Studio with your Build ID before submitting again.

7. Contact

For questions, contact studio@shashwataneja.com.
