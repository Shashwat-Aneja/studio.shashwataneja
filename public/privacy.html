STUDIO / PRIVACY POLICY

Your information,
handled with care.

Effective 14 November 2026 · Version studio-privacy-2026-11-14-v3

Preview review notice: The operator's public business address, formal grievance designation and retention schedule still require confirmation before a public commercial launch. This policy describes the implemented preview; it is not a compliance certification.

1. Operator and contact

Studio is operated by Shashwat Aneja. Contact studio@shashwataneja.com for project, privacy, correction, deletion or grievance requests. Include your Build ID if available. A public postal business address and formally designated grievance contact have not yet been confirmed for publication.

2. Information and purposes

The booking form collects your name, email, phone, service or custom-project choices, additional requirements, terms acknowledgment and adult self-declaration. Studio calculates an indicative price/timeline and generates a UUID Build ID and provisional BUILD DETAILS PDF. These details let Studio review your request, prepare a quotation, contact you, and send a notification to Studio plus an acknowledgment and PDF to the email you supply. No marketing subscription is created. Do not send passwords, card details, government IDs, health information or other sensitive personal data.

3. Request processing and legal grounds

You voluntarily provide details for the specific purpose of requesting services and receiving the related acknowledgment. Accepting the service terms is separate from the adult declaration and this privacy notice. There is no marketing-consent checkbox. Studio does not treat a terms checkbox as blanket consent for unrelated uses. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular use, that consent must be separately obtained. India's DPDP framework has phased commencement; future statutory duties and rights will apply as their provisions commence. The legal audit distinguishes current requirements from preparation for those duties.

4. Emails, PDFs and providers

Vercel hosts the site and processes requests and operational logs. Resend sends the two transactional emails and attachments. Cloudflare Email Routing forwards Studio's incoming mail to the operator's Google Gmail inbox. The customer email address is used for its acknowledgment and as the reply address on the Studio notification. The same provisional PDF contains your submitted project details and contact information, so check that you control the email address before submitting. Email addresses are syntax-checked, not independently verified for ownership or deliverability.

Images on public pages are requested from Unsplash's image host, which receives normal connection information such as your IP address. The website does not intentionally put contact details in image URLs. The PDF uses bundled assets rather than fetching customer-supplied URLs. Resend open/click tracking was disabled in the inspected sending-domain configuration.

5. Locations and transfers

Providers may process information outside India. The inspected Vercel preview runs in the United States and Resend's sending domain uses Japan; Cloudflare and Google have global infrastructure. These observations do not establish every storage, support or backup location. Provider contracts, transfer safeguards and any applicable legal restrictions require owner review before production. No promise of India-only data storage is made.

6. Browser storage and cookies

Local storage remembers the selected visual theme. Session storage holds a Build ID, timestamp, signed retry token and signed message-acceptance receipts, indexed by a hash of the form details. It does not hold the raw name, email, phone, notes or PDF; the hash/token can still be linkable to your request and are not claimed to be anonymous. They support unchanged retries in the same browser session. You can clear browser storage, but doing so may lose retry protection. Preview access and hosting/security services may set their own protection cookies. No advertising or analytics script was found in the application source examined; platform settings and future changes require separate review.

7. Retention and deletion

The application does not create a booking database. Request data and PDFs are processed temporarily by the server, then retained in the relevant email systems, customer inboxes and provider logs/backups. No automatic email-deletion schedule has been configured or verified by this audit. The owner still needs to approve and configure a retention schedule that balances the service purpose with applicable legal holds and record-keeping duties. Resend's duplicate-protection window is not a data-retention policy. Deleting an email or clearing browser storage does not necessarily erase all provider copies or backups.

8. Security and incidents

Server-side validation, signed retry details, bounded requests, restrictive browser headers and scoped credentials reduce risk. Preview rate limiting is configured; production protection requires its own reviewed rollout. No system is guaranteed secure. Suspected disclosure or credential compromise should be reported to the contact above. Studio must assess affected data, contain the issue and make any notifications required by applicable law; this policy does not claim that monitoring, a statutory contact registration or every incident procedure is already operational.

9. Choices, rights and grievances

Contact Studio to request access to your correspondence, correction, cessation of follow-up, or deletion. Use the address originally supplied and the Build ID where possible; Studio should verify requests proportionately without routinely asking for government IDs. Any legally required retention or third-party limitations should be explained. Where processing relies on consent, you may request withdrawal; that does not erase records that must lawfully be retained. Statutory DPDP access, correction/erasure, grievance and nomination rights apply when the relevant provisions commence and their conditions are met. This preview does not claim an active Data Protection Board complaint workflow for uncommenced substantive duties.

If a consumer grievance is unresolved, you may use applicable statutory remedies, including the National Consumer Helpline and competent consumer fora. This does not waive rights or impose an exclusive dispute channel. Specific statutory grievance deadlines, where applicable, are addressed in the owner runbook and require an assigned person and operating process.

10. Adults and children's information

Booking requests are restricted to people aged 18 or older who are legally capable of contracting. The declaration is unchecked by default and checked by the server. It is not reliable age or identity verification and is not verifiable parental consent. If you are under 18, do not submit the form; an adult may contact Studio using their own details. Do not supply a child's personal information in project notes. If Studio discovers a child's information, it should stop unnecessary processing and review deletion or a lawful guardian-assisted process before further use. No date of birth, ID document or government ID is requested.

11. Changes

Material changes to purposes or providers require this notice to be reviewed. The version supplied with a request is included in its email/PDF record; this is not an independently verified consent ledger. Contact Studio with questions.
