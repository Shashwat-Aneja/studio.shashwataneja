STUDIO / PRIVACY NOTICE

Your information,
handled with care.

Review draft: This notice must be reviewed by the site owner before live booking and payment collection is enabled.

1. Who operates Studio?

Studio is operated by Shashwat Aneja. For privacy questions, contact studio@shashwataneja.com.

2. Information collected

Exploring services and generating an indicative project outline is designed not to require an account. If you proceed to a booking, the flow may request your name, email address, phone number and optional project notes, along with the project requirements and estimate needed to prepare the booking.

3. How information is used

  • To prepare and respond to project enquiries.
  • To generate a scope estimate and delivery outline.
  • If checkout is enabled, to create and verify a booking and provide project follow-up.
  • To protect the service and investigate payment or security issues.

4. Service providers

If online payments are enabled, payment details are handled by the selected payment provider. Studio should not collect or store full card credentials. Booking and project records may be stored in a secured database after payment is verified. Provider names and any analytics providers must be listed here before launch.

5. Storage and security

Studio is designed to create permanent client and project records only after the server verifies a payment-provider webhook. Security controls reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Never send passwords, card details or other sensitive information in project notes.

6. Retention and your choices

Information should be retained only as long as needed for project delivery, accounting, legal obligations and dispute handling. A final retention schedule and process for access, correction or deletion requests must be confirmed by the operator before launch. Contact the email above for privacy requests.

7. Changes

This notice may be updated as Studio's booking and service-provider configuration changes. The effective date should be added after owner review.
