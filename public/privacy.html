Email-only booking requests · Updated 14 November 2026

1. Who operates Studio?

Studio is operated by Shashwat Aneja. For privacy questions, contact studio@shashwataneja.com.

2. Information collected

A booking request contains your name, email address, phone number, project choices, additional requirements, indicative estimate and acknowledgement of the service terms. No account or payment information is required. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information in project notes.

3. How information is used

Studio uses these details to prepare a provisional BUILD DETAILS PDF, review your requirements, contact you and agree a quotation. Submitting a request does not confirm a project or payment.

4. Service providers

Vercel hosts the website and processes server requests. Resend processes the booking notification and attached PDF. Cloudflare routes incoming Studio email to the owner's Gmail inbox, provided by Google. Those providers process information as part of hosting, sending, routing and storing email. Their own privacy policies also apply.

5. Storage and security

This booking workflow does not write customer records to a Studio database. Request details and PDFs are processed temporarily by the server and retained in the relevant email systems. Hosting and email providers may retain operational logs. Your browser's session storage holds a Build ID, timestamp and signed retry token, indexed by a hash of the form details; the application does not store your name, email, phone or notes there. Session storage helps reuse the Build ID if you retry the same details in the same browser session. API credentials remain on the server. Security controls reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention and your choices

Studio retains correspondence as needed to review and deliver projects and handle related obligations. You can contact Studio to ask about access, correction or deletion of your information. A detailed retention schedule remains subject to owner review. Do not submit information you do not want included in the owner notification and PDF.

7. Changes

This notice may be updated when the workflow or providers change.
